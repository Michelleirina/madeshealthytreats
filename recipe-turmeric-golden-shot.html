Turmeric Lemon Sips

An earthy turmeric-and-lemon drink with optional honey. Use a small amount of turmeric and dilute to taste.

About 2 small servingsAbout 5 minutes

By Made · Made and tasted at home

What you will need

  1. ¼ teaspoon ground turmeric
  2. ½ cup cold water
  3. 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  4. 1 teaspoon honey (optional)
  5. A tiny pinch of black pepper (optional)

How to make it

1

Put the water in a clean glass or small pitcher.

2

Add the other ingredients and stir or whisk until mixed.

3

Taste a small amount and add water if too sharp. Divide into servings and stir again before drinking.

What's in this drink

  • Ground turmeric: Among the nutrients in 100 g of reference ground turmeric, USDA lists 19.8 mg manganese and 55 mg iron. USDA: Spices, turmeric, ground.
  • Lemon juice: Among the nutrients in 100 g of reference lemon juice, USDA lists 38.7 mg vitamin C and 20 mcg folate (DFE). USDA: Lemon juice, raw.

Nutrient figures are per 100 g of the USDA reference ingredient, not per serving.

Straining and dilution change this turmeric lemon sips, so fiber or minerals listed for raw ingredients cannot be assumed to remain in the same amounts. The turmeric numbers for turmeric lemon sips refer to ground turmeric, and a small spice measure is far less than 100 g.

Storage

Cover and refrigerate leftovers promptly. Follow the storage directions on opened packaged ingredients. FoodSafety.gov recommends refrigerating perishable food within two hours, or one hour above 90°F, and keeping the refrigerator at 40°F or below.

Short FAQ

What should I check before starting?

Whisk turmeric into a little water before adding the rest. It can settle, so stir before serving. Pepper is a flavor option, not a required absorption promise.

Can I change an ingredient?

For a sweeter citrus version, replace part of the water with orange juice. Leave out black pepper if you dislike it.
