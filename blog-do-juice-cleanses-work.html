Do Juice Cleanses Work? Evidence, Limits, and a Better Use for Juice

May 8, 2026 · 10 min

If you enjoy fresh juice, you do not need to turn it into a cleanse. A carrot-orange drink can be pleasant with lunch. That does not establish that drinking only juice for several days removes toxins or creates lasting weight loss.

What the evidence actually says

The NIH National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health reports that research on detox programs in people is limited and often low quality. Its summary notes that there is no compelling research supporting detox diets for weight management or removing toxins. A short-term change on the scale is not proof of cleansing.

Juice and a juice-only diet are different choices

Adding a small glass of juice to a meal is different from replacing all food with liquid. Strained juice leaves much of the pulp behind. A juice-only routine can also crowd out other foods, including sources of protein and fat. Nutrition depends on the whole eating pattern, not the number of colorful bottles in the fridge.

Why a cleanse may feel convincing

A tightly structured plan can make decisions feel easier. Eating differently can also change how you feel. Neither observation proves that toxins were removed. A personal story, especially one with several lifestyle changes at once, cannot tell you which part produced an outcome.

Safety deserves attention

NCCIH warns that severely restrictive diets may not provide needed nutrients and that untreated juices can cause illness. People with diabetes should discuss major eating changes with their health-care team. Fresh homemade juice is unpasteurized, so clean equipment and produce handling matter. People at higher risk from foodborne illness should follow guidance about pasteurized juice.

Enjoy juice without the cleanse

Make one flavor you like and serve it as a drink alongside food. You can choose a smaller glass, dilute a strong blend with water, or leave it unstrained for more body. There is no need to force down a bitter drink because a label calls it detox.

For a bright combination, blend cucumber, apple, and a squeeze of lemon. For a fuller fruit drink, leave the pulp in. Our blender juice guide explains the method and gives three combinations.

What to take away from a cleanse claim

Ask what the claim means, what was measured, and whether the evidence applies to the product being sold. Words such as reset, clean, and flush often sound specific without naming a verifiable outcome. A useful recipe should explain what it tastes like and how to make it, with health claims tied to evidence.
