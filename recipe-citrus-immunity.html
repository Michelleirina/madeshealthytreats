Citrus Immunity Shot

I make this when everyone around me starts sniffling and I want something that tastes bold enough to wake up my whole face. The first version was honestly too spicy, because I got brave with cayenne and paid for it. Now it is bright, warm, and balanced. It feels like sunshine with a little attitude.

160 calories5 minutes1 serving

Why I Love This One

I love this drink because it is small but mighty. The orange and lemon are juicy and sharp, the ginger brings warmth, and honey rounds everything out so it still feels friendly.

When I make it in the morning, I pour it into a small glass and sip it slowly instead of tossing it back. It feels like a tiny promise to myself that I am paying attention.

Once the flavors are in your head, the rest is beautifully simple. Here is exactly how I make it in my own kitchen.

Here's What You'll Need

  1. One peeled orange
  2. The juice of one lemon
  3. One half cup of cold water
  4. One inch piece of fresh ginger, peeled
  5. One teaspoon of raw honey
  6. One tiny pinch of cayenne pepper
  7. A few ice cubes

I like to gather everything before I start blending, because smoothies move fast once the blender is out.

Let's Blend It Together

1

Add the cold water to the blender first so the citrus can blend smoothly.

2

Place the orange, lemon juice, ginger, honey, cayenne, and ice into the blender.

3

Blend until the ginger is fully broken down and the drink looks bright and frothy.

4

Taste carefully and add a little more honey if the lemon is very sharp.

5

Pour into a small glass and sip while it is cold and fresh.

Made's Tips

Start with the smallest pinch of cayenne, because a little truly goes a long way.

If you prefer a smoother texture, strain the drink through a fine mesh sieve.

Use fresh lemon juice because bottled lemon never gives the same sunny flavor.

May this one help you feel bright, brave, and cared for. Love, Made
