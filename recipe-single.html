Green Detox Bliss

A cold, crisp, lightly sweet green smoothie that resets your morning without slowing you down.

180 calories5 minutes1 serving

Ingredients

🥬2 big handfuls fresh baby spinach
🥒1 Persian cucumber, roughly chopped
🍌1 frozen banana
🫚1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled
🍋juice of 1 lemon
🥥1 cup cold coconut water
🧊handful of ice

Instructions

1

Add coconut water and spinach to blender first — this helps it blend smoothly.

2

Add cucumber, frozen banana, and ginger.

3

Squeeze in lemon juice and add ice.

4

Blend on high for 60–90 seconds until completely smooth.

5

Taste and adjust — add more lemon for tang or more banana for sweetness.

6

Pour over ice into a tall glass. Serve immediately for maximum nutrients.
