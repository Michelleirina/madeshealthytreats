Privacy and Cookies

How this site uses browser storage, hosting, and Google services.

Updated September 29, 2026.

Saved recipes

The Save recipe feature stores the recipe title and page link in local storage in your browser. It does not create an account or synchronize your collection across devices. You can remove individual recipes from the saved page or clear the site's browser data to remove the collection.

Analytics and advertising services

Our pages include Google Analytics and Google AdSense scripts. These services may receive information such as the page URL, IP address, browser details, cookies, or other identifiers when a page loads. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this or other websites.

Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's privacy policy. You can manage advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center and use your browser's cookie and site-data controls. Advertising behavior depends on the services and settings in use; the presence of a script does not mean ads are currently approved or displayed.

Hosting and external links

The website is hosted by Netlify. Hosting services may process request information when serving pages. See Netlify's privacy policy. Following a social or source link takes you to another service with its own privacy practices.

Messages

The contact page links to Instagram. Messages sent there are handled through that service, not a contact form on this website. Please do not send sensitive medical information.

Questions and updates

Use the contact page for questions about this site's features. This page will be updated when the site's data handling changes.
