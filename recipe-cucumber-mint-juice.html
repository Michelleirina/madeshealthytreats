Cucumber Mint Juice

This cucumber mint juice might be the most refreshing thing I have ever made. I first blended it on one of those hot afternoons when the air felt thick and my body felt a little bloated and annoyed. Cucumber, mint, lime, and coconut water sounded almost too simple, but the first sip made me stand quietly in the kitchen like I had discovered a secret door. It is cold, clean, and ridiculously easy to love.

90 calories5 minutes1 mason jar

Why I Juice This One

I juice this one because cucumber is hydrating, mint feels cooling, and lime makes the whole jar sparkle. Coconut water gives it a gentle electrolyte boost without making it heavy.

When I make this after a salty meal or a long hot day, I feel lighter and calmer. It is not a meal replacement for me. It is a refreshing reset that helps me stop confusing thirst with hunger.

Here's What You'll Need

You will need one large cucumber, one green apple cored, a generous handful of fresh mint leaves, the juice of one lime, one cup of coconut water, and a handful of ice.

How I Make It

I chop the cucumber and apple, then add them to the blender with mint, lime juice, coconut water, and ice. I blend on high for about 60 seconds until the mint is fully broken down and the mixture smells like a spa day with better snacks.

I strain it through a fine mesh strainer when I want it silky, pressing with a spoon until the juice is clear and pale green. Then I pour it into a mason jar over extra ice and drink it immediately because mint tastes best when it is fresh.

Made's Tips

Use a chilled cucumber if you can, because this juice is happiest when it is very cold.

Do not be shy with the mint if you love that cooling flavor the way I do.

If you want it less sweet, use half a green apple and add an extra squeeze of lime.
