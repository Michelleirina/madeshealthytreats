Beet Juice: Earthy Flavor, Easy Pairings, and Research Limits

May 8, 2026 · 10 min

Beet juice has a deep color and an earthy taste that can surprise you. Apple and citrus make it more approachable, while ginger gives a sharper finish. Start with a small amount of beet if you are trying it for the first time; you can always add more.

How to balance the earthy flavor

For a blender drink, try ½ small beet, 1 cored apple, ½ cup water, and a squeeze of lemon. Wash the produce, peel the beet if you prefer, and grate or chop it finely. Blend thoroughly and strain if you want a thinner texture. Raw beet is firm, so check that your blender can handle it.

Orange can replace some of the apple for a more citrus-forward drink. Use a thin slice of ginger rather than a large chunk. If beet still dominates, dilute the drink or use less beet in the next batch. Stronger is not automatically better.

What the blood-pressure research can tell us

Beetroot juice has been studied for blood-pressure effects. A 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis examined randomized trials in hypertension. That is research on particular participants and interventions, not proof that this apple-and-beet recipe will produce the same outcome.

A homemade batch does not have a standardized nitrate content, and a recipe page cannot assess your health or treatment needs. Do not replace prescribed treatment with beet juice. If blood pressure is why you are considering concentrated beetroot products, discuss that choice with your clinician.

Is it an energy drink?

The word energy can mean calories, alertness, or exercise performance. Those are different questions. A brightly colored drink does not establish that it will prevent tiredness or improve a workout. If you enjoy it, serve it as food rather than relying on an outcome the recipe has not measured.

Prevent a stained counter

Cut beet on a washable board, keep a bowl nearby for scraps, and wipe splashes promptly. Strain over a bowl wide enough to catch drips. Rinse equipment after serving so dried pulp does not become a second chore.

A practical place to start

Our beet apple carrot juice gives a full ingredient list. For a lighter vegetable flavor, try cucumber mint juice. Both are recipes to adapt to your taste, without a promise about your health.
