Three steps from produce to glass

Prepare small pieces

Wash produce and remove seeds, cores, or peel as needed. Grate firm vegetables if your blender struggles.

Blend with liquid

Add water first, then produce. Stop before scraping or stirring, and add liquid if the blades cannot move the mixture.

Choose your texture

Strain for a thinner drink or keep the pulp for more body. Taste, adjust, and serve promptly.
