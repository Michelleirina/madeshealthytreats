How to Make Fresh Juice Without a Juicer

This is the little blender method that made fresh juice feel possible in my real kitchen.

1

Blend Until Bright

I add the chopped produce and cold water to my blender, then blend everything on high for 60 seconds until the mixture looks smooth, colorful, and fully broken down.

2

Strain It Slowly

I pour the blended mixture through a fine mesh strainer or nut milk bag set over a bowl, then let the juice collect while I press gently.

3

Serve It Cold

I squeeze well, pour the fresh juice over ice into a mason jar, and drink it right away because that first cold sip is always the best one.
