Guava Strawberry Smoothie

Fragrant pink guava and strawberries make a rosy smoothie with a short ingredient list. Seedless guava pulp keeps preparation simple, and plain yogurt adds creaminess without bringing banana into the mix.

About 10 minutesSmoothie
Gather your ingredients →

By Made · Made and tasted at home

What you will need

Yield: 2 small glasses (about 2 cups). Final volume changes with fruit, straining, and liquid adjustments.

  • ½ cup unsweetened seedless guava pulp (about 120 g)
  • 1 cup hulled strawberries (about 140 g), fresh or frozen
  • ✦½ cup plain yogurt (about 120 g)
  • ✦½ cup cold water (120 ml), plus more as needed
  • ✦Optional: 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup, to taste

How to make it

1

Measure unsweetened seedless guava pulp. If using a frozen product, follow its thawing and handling directions so you can measure a manageable portion.

2

Wash fresh strawberries under running water and remove their leafy tops. Measure the prepared berries. For frozen strawberries, follow the package and blender instructions.

3

Add water and yogurt to the blender first, then guava pulp and strawberries. Blend until smooth, stopping the machine before scraping down the sides if necessary.

4

Taste before adding optional sweetener. Add water a tablespoon at a time if the mixture is thicker than you want, and blend briefly after each small adjustment.

5

Pour into two small glasses and serve promptly. The color can be pale pink or deeper rose depending on the guava pulp and the berries.

What's in this drink

  • Guava: A 100 g amount of reference guava is recorded with 228 mg vitamin C and 0.23 mg copper. USDA: Guavas, common, raw.
  • Strawberries: A 100 g amount of reference strawberries is recorded with 58.8 mg vitamin C and 0.386 mg manganese. USDA: Strawberries, raw.
  • Plain dairy yogurt: A 100 g amount of reference plain dairy yogurt is recorded with 0.37 mcg vitamin B12 and 0.142 mg riboflavin (B2). USDA: Yogurt, plain, whole milk.

Nutrient figures are per 100 g of the USDA reference ingredient, not per serving.

The yogurt entry for guava strawberry smoothie describes whole-milk dairy yogurt, not a plant-based alternative. The guava record describes raw fruit; seedless commercial pulp can have a different composition.

Storage

Cover and refrigerate leftovers promptly. Follow the storage directions on opened packaged ingredients. FoodSafety.gov recommends refrigerating perishable food within two hours, or one hour above 90°F, and keeping the refrigerator at 40°F or below.

Short FAQ

Can I use bottled guava nectar?

Nectar is not the measured pulp ingredient. It changes both liquid and sweetness, so use a smaller trial and reduce the added water if experimenting rather than treating it as a one-for-one swap.

Does this recipe need banana?

No. The measured recipe uses guava, strawberries, yogurt, and water. It is written to keep those fruit flavors clear.

Why is my smoothie a different shade of pink?

Guava variety, berries, yogurt, and dilution all affect appearance.
