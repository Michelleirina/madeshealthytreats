Peanut Butter Banana Cacao Smoothie

Creamy banana, peanut butter, and a little cacao make a familiar, dessert-like flavor without a complicated ingredient list.

1 large glassAbout 5 minutes

By Made · Made and tasted at home

What you will need

  1. One cup of oat milk
  2. One large frozen banana
  3. One tablespoon of natural peanut butter
  4. One teaspoon of cacao powder
  5. One teaspoon of honey
  6. One tablespoon of rolled oats
  7. A small handful of ice

How to make it

1

Wash fresh produce unless labeled ready-to-eat. Remove pits, hard seeds, or peel as needed, and cut large pieces to suit your blender.

2

Add the liquid first, then softer ingredients and fruit. Add frozen pieces gradually if your blender struggles.

3

Blend until smooth. Stop the blender before scraping or stirring, and add a little liquid if the mixture is too thick.

4

Taste before adding optional sweetener or extra citrus. Divide into glasses and serve.

What's in this drink

  • Peanut butter: On the USDA 100 g reference basis, peanut butter includes 1.66 mg manganese and 9.1 mg vitamin E (alpha-tocopherol). USDA: Peanut butter, smooth style, without salt.
  • Banana: On the USDA 100 g reference basis, banana includes 0.367 mg vitamin B6 and 0.27 mg manganese. USDA: Bananas, raw.
  • Rolled oats: On the USDA 100 g reference basis, rolled oats includes 4.92 mg manganese and 0.626 mg copper. USDA: Oats (Includes foods for USDA's Food Distribution Program).

Nutrient figures are per 100 g of the USDA reference ingredient, not per serving.

Storage

Cover and refrigerate leftovers promptly. Follow the storage directions on opened packaged ingredients. FoodSafety.gov recommends refrigerating perishable food within two hours, or one hour above 90°F, and keeping the refrigerator at 40°F or below.

Short FAQ

What should I check before starting?

Oats add body and may need a longer blend. Start with fewer ice cubes if the banana is frozen solid.

Can I change an ingredient?

Sunflower seed butter can replace peanut butter if suitable for your needs. Use dairy or soy milk if you prefer it. Taste before adding honey.
